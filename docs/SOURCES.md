## Cultural story

The Kadayawan Village at Magsaysay Park presents houses of five Indigenous and six Moro groups according to the City Government report above. Department of Tourism context: https://www.tourism.gov.ph/destination/davao-region/davao-city/ . Copy describes the village without asserting this year's program or appropriating community-specific patterns.
